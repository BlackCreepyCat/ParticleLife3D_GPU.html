Setups

The JSON file holds the settings and the matrix in plain text, editable by hand. It is interchangeable with the WebGPU build.

Compute cost grows with the square of the range: past 4000 particles, lower the range toward 0.05 to stay at 60 frames per second.

Mouse: hold left click to attract, right click to repel.
View: wheel zooms on the cursor, middle click or Shift + drag pans, double-click reframes.
Keys: Space pauses, R respawns, M draws a new matrix, 0 reframes, H hides the panel.
